# Name review

*v2, 2026-09-25, for the **general** random-thought organizer. 22 names were screened
against the US App Store and Google Play, USPTO (tmsearch wordmarks, including phonetic variants), RDAP for .app/.com,
npm, the MCP registry, the Anthropic directory and GitHub. The work was then adversarially re-checked: 25
claims, 19 confirmed, 6 corrected. Raw data:
[`../research/raw/2026-09-25-research-v2-general.json`](../research/raw/2026-09-25-research-v2-general.json).
v1 (conversation-prep names) is in [`../research/raw/2026-09-25-research.json`](../research/raw/2026-09-25-research.json).*

> **Not legal trademark clearance.** International registers (EUIPO/UKIPO/WIPO)
> have not been searched.

## Recommendation

**Brand: Mullwise.** **Descriptor: "your random thought organizer".** **Handle: `/mullwise`.**
**Internal codename: RTO** (never public).

> *Mullwise: your random thought organizer. Drop a thought; it connects the dots.*

| Check (2026-09-25) | Mullwise |
|---|---|
| US App Store / Google Play | No app ("No results for mullwise") |
| USPTO | 0 filings for MULLWISE, MULL WISE, MULWISE, MULLWIZE or `mullwis*`. No live MULL-formative mark in software or SaaS (live ones: trailer hitches, clothing, music recordings). MULLED marks are food, drink, candles and cosmetics. |
| Domains | **mullwise.app, .com, .ai, .io all unregistered** (RDAP 404) |
| npm / MCP registry / GitHub | `mullwise` is free on npm, has no MCP registry hits, and the GitHub user/org handle is free |

**Why this name:**
- "Mull" is what you do with a stray thought, and "-wise" is the clarity the AI adds.
- It fits every subject kind (topic, person, talk, decision, project), not just conversations.
- It keeps your earlier "Mulled" instinct, whose .app was taken, as a coined word you can own.

**Risks:**
- **"MULL – Save & Resurface"** (Google Play) is a privacy-first, on-device save-and-resurface
  app that shows as "Mull" in the Android share sheet. That's close positioning. **Use `/mullwise`, not `/mull`**, and
  expect some confusion if you ship on Android.
- The short "mull" domains are gone (mull.app registered, mull.ai for sale).
- "-wise" is a common software suffix (Splitwise, Typewise), which is slightly less distinctive.
- The name doesn't say "notes", so always pair it with the descriptor.

## Why not "RTO" publicly (still true)

- **Return to Office:** everyday workplace term.
- **Recovery Time Objective:** a common term among the developer and IT audience that installs Claude plugins.
- **India's Regional Transport Office:** fills 13 of 13 Google Play results and all of App Store page 1 for "RTO".
- **Live "RTO" marks:** Rohde & Schwarz in class 9, and 360training.com in classes 9 and 41.

## Before any public launch

1. Register **mullwise.app** and **mullwise.com** (plus .ai/.io if wanted). Nothing was purchased during research.
2. Claim the **`mullwise`** GitHub org, the npm package name, and the MCP registry namespace when you publish.
3. Run a USPTO search in classes 9, 42 and 35 including design marks, plus EUIPO, UKIPO and WIPO. Consider an attorney
   knockout search if you will sell it.

**Decision (Andrew, 2026-09-25): Mullwise.** The repo, plugin, packages and MCP server are
named `mullwise`. RTO stays the internal codename.
